Before you start

Quick terms

  1. One private chat. Your link works once. The chat ends for both of you when either person leaves, switches off camera, mic or location, or loses connection.
  2. Access. Camera, mic and location stay on during the chat and are shared with the other person. We may also ask to show alerts and keep your screen awake.
  3. We keep nothing. No accounts, messages, calls, files or locations are stored, and we run no analytics, ads, tracking or logs of our own. Only a random room ID is kept for up to 24 hours so a used link can’t be reused.
  4. Where it runs. Hosted on Cloudflare, which as the infrastructure provider may briefly record technical data such as IP addresses and connection times under its own policies.
  5. Direct connection. Calls and movies go straight between your browsers, so the other person’s browser may see your network address. They can also screenshot or record, so chat only with people you trust. Movie links load from the video site itself (like YouTube), which can see you watching.
  6. Fair use. Don’t use it for anything illegal or to harm others. It’s provided as is, and a chat can end at any time.
