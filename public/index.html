Before you start

Quick terms

  1. One private chat. Your link works once. The chat ends for both of you the moment either person leaves, turns off the camera, mic or location, or loses connection.
  2. Access we need. Camera, microphone and location stay on during the chat and are shared with the other person. We’ll also ask to show alerts (optional) and keep your screen awake.
  3. We keep nothing. No accounts, messages, calls, files or locations are stored. We run no analytics, ads or tracking and keep no logs of our own. Only a random room ID is kept for up to 24 hours so a used link can’t be reused.
  4. Where it runs. This app is hosted on Cloudflare. As the infrastructure provider, Cloudflare may briefly record technical data such as IP addresses and connection times under its own policies, outside our control.
  5. Direct connection. Video goes straight between your browsers, so the other person’s browser may see your network address. They can also screenshot or record, so only chat with people you trust.
  6. Use it fairly. Don’t use it for anything illegal or to harm others. The service is provided as is, without guarantees, and a chat can end at any time.
